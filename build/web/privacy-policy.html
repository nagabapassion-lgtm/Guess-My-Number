Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "Guess My Number" (the "App") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the App. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account email address and display name. We do not request your password.
  • Game data: When you play a multiplayer game, we store the room code, your guesses, the outcome of the game (win/loss), and the time the game was played. This data is associated with your user ID so you can see your game history.
  • Leaderboard data: When you complete the Daily Challenge, your display name, guess count, and completion time appear on the public daily leaderboard.
  • Anonymous usage: If you choose "Continue as guest", we generate an anonymous user ID. No personally identifiable information is collected in this mode.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the game functionality (multiplayer rooms, daily challenge, game history)
  • Display your name on leaderboards and in multiplayer rooms
  • Associate your game history with your account so you can view it across devices
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the game (preventing cheating and abuse)

We do not use your information for advertising, sell your information to third parties, or share it with anyone except as required by law.

3. How We Store Your Information

Your data is stored in Google Firebase, a cloud platform provided by Google. Firebase uses industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your data is stored in the European Union (europe-west region).

Authentication data (your Google account email and display name) is managed by Firebase Authentication. Game data and leaderboard data are stored in Cloud Firestore.

4. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion at any time (see Section 6).
  • Game data: Multiplayer game records are retained indefinitely so you can view your full game history. You can request deletion of specific games.
  • Leaderboard data: Daily Challenge entries are retained indefinitely so the historical leaderboard remains accurate.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Firestore database, and hosting. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google Sign-In — For account authentication. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — For hosting the App's web files. See Vercel Privacy Policy.

We do not use any advertising networks, analytics services (other than what Firebase provides by default), or tracking pixels.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You can request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Deletion: You can request that we delete your account and all associated game data.
  • Correction: You can update your display name by signing in with Google and changing your Google profile name.
  • Opt-out: You can play as a guest at any time, in which case no personally identifiable information is collected.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
